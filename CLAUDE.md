# Project guide

Reply to the maintainer in Spanish.

## Overview

Daily sales forecasting for a sports retailer during the autumn campaign (late October to November), which includes Black Friday. The project trains a `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` on 2021-2024 sales (24 products), then forecasts November 2025 and exposes the result in a Streamlit simulator that predicts recursively (each predicted day feeds the lags of the next one).

Use the libraries pinned in `requirements.txt`.

## Data and variables

Variables available in the notebooks:

- `ventas_df`: ['fecha', 'producto_id', 'nombre', 'categoria', 'subcategoria', 'precio_base', 'es_estrella', 'unidades_vendidas', 'precio_venta', 'ingresos']
- `competencia_df`: ['fecha', 'producto_id', 'Amazon', 'Decathlon', 'Deporvillage']
- `df` (81 columns): ['fecha', 'producto_id', 'nombre', 'categoria', 'subcategoria', 'precio_base', 'es_estrella', 'unidades_vendidas', 'precio_venta', 'ingresos', 'año', 'mes', 'dia_mes', 'dia_semana', 'dia_año', 'semana_año', 'trimestre', 'es_fin_semana', 'es_festivo', 'es_vispera_festivo', 'es_black_friday', 'es_cyber_monday', 'es_semana_black_friday', 'dias_hasta_black_friday', 'es_inicio_mes', 'es_fin_mes', 'unidades_lag_1', 'unidades_lag_2', 'unidades_lag_3', 'unidades_lag_4', 'unidades_lag_5', 'unidades_lag_6', 'unidades_lag_7', 'unidades_media_movil_7', 'descuento_porcentaje', 'precio_competencia', 'ratio_precio'], plus 44 one-hot columns starting with `nombre_h_`, `categoria_h_` and `subcategoria_h_` (full list in `models/onehot_columnas.csv`).
- Amazon, Decathlon and Deporvillage are no longer in `df`; they only exist in `competencia_df`.
- `train_df` and `validation_df`: `df` split by year. `train_df` covers 2021-2023 (2,160 rows) and `validation_df` covers 2024 (720 rows).
- `target` (`"unidades_vendidas"`) and `predictoras`: the 74 predictors (all columns of `df` except `fecha`, `ingresos`, the target and the text columns).
- `modelo`: conservative `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` trained on `train_df`. `modelo_final`: same parameters, trained on the 2,880 rows of `df`.
- `metricas(y_real, y_pred)`: returns MAE, RMSE, MAPE (%), WMAPE (%) and R2.
- `inferencia_df` (`notebooks/forecasting.ipynb`): same 81 columns as `df`, 720 rows (November 2025, 24 products), with `unidades_vendidas` and `ingresos` empty, and with the lags and the moving average empty for most of November.
- `cols_onehot` (list of the 44 one-hot columns) and `aplicar_onehot(df_nuevo)` (applies the saved one-hot encoding to new data).

## Files

- `data/raw/entrenamiento/` (`ventas.csv`, `competencia.csv`) and `data/raw/inferencia/` (`ventas_2025_inferencia.csv`): synthetic source data, versioned.
- `data/processed/df.csv` and `data/processed/inferencia_df_transformado.csv`: generated by the notebooks.
- `data/processed/df_sin_onehot.csv` (2,880 rows, 37 columns: `df` right before the one-hot encoding, generated by `notebooks/entrenamiento.ipynb`) and `data/processed/inferencia_df_sin_onehot.csv` (720 rows, same 37 columns, November 2025 with the NaN of the lags and the moving average, generated by `notebooks/forecasting.ipynb`): the input of the scikit-learn Pipelines, which do their own one-hot encoding.
- `models/modelo_final.joblib` and `models/onehot_columnas.csv`: generated by `notebooks/entrenamiento.ipynb`.
- `models/pipeline_final.joblib` (conservative Pipeline: `OneHotEncoder` + `HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, trained on the 2,880 rows) is generated by `notebooks/modelado_pipeline.ipynb`; `models/pipeline_final_tuned.joblib` (same Pipeline with the Optuna hyperparameters, seed 42) is generated by `notebooks/tuning.ipynb`.
- `config/best_params.json` (versioned): best hyperparameters, search context, `adopted` flag and `adoption_note`.
- `notebooks/entrenamiento.ipynb` (EDA, features, validation, final model), `notebooks/forecasting.ipynb` (2025 inference data), `notebooks/modelado_pipeline.ipynb` (Pipeline and recursive evaluation on 2024), `notebooks/tuning.ipynb` (Optuna search and seed check), `notebooks/forecast_sanity_checks.ipynb` (coherence checks of the 2025 forecast) and `app/streamlit_app.py` (simulator).
- `src/forecasting.py`: `recursive_forecast(pipeline, df_dias)`, recursive prediction of all the products day by day. `src/simulation.py`: `apply_price_plan(datos, ventana, descuento, factor_competencia)`, applies a discount only on the days of a window ("plan", "black_friday", "black_friday_week" or "november"; Black Friday 2025 is day 28 and its week is days 24-30) and the competition factor on the whole month.
- `tests/test_forecasting.py` and `tests/test_simulation.py`: run them with `python -m pytest` from the project root.

Notebook execution order (kernel `forecasting`): `entrenamiento` -> `forecasting` -> `modelado_pipeline` -> `tuning` -> `forecast_sanity_checks` (optional).

## Conventions

- Lags and the moving average are computed per product and per year, with `shift(1)` so the current day is never used.
- The recursion must never use the current day: on each day after the first, `unidades_lag_1` is the previous day's prediction and the moving average is the mean of the 7 updated lags.
- Validation is by year (train 2021-2023, validation 2024), never a random split.
- The app always loads `models/pipeline_final_tuned.joblib`.
- `data/processed/` and `models/` are not versioned; they are regenerated by running the notebooks.
- The data in `data/raw/` is synthetic.
